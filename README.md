## 🚀 Selected Work

<br/>

### 🧠 Neuronest — AI + VR Mental Health Therapy
![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Active_Startup-6e40c9?style=flat-square)
![Stack](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3776AB?style=flat-square&logo=python&logoColor=white)
![Stack](https://img.shields.io/badge/VR-Adaptive_AI-black?style=flat-square)

India's mental health crisis is real — therapists are scarce, stigma is high, and PTSD treatment is out of reach for millions. Neuronest is an AI-driven VR therapy platform with an adaptive emotional feedback loop: the virtual environment responds in real time to the user's physiological and behavioral signals.

*Not archived. We're still building this.*

<br/>

### 🌊 Jal Drishti — AI Flood Prediction
![Award](https://img.shields.io/badge/🥇_Winner-Geospatial_Hackathon_2026-FFD700?style=flat-square)
![Stack](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3776AB?style=flat-square&logo=python&logoColor=white)
![Stack](https://img.shields.io/badge/Geospatial_AI-Scikit--learn-F7931E?style=flat-square&logo=scikit-learn&logoColor=white)

Urban floods in India kill hundreds every monsoon — not because the disaster is unavoidable, but because response is always reactive. Jal Drishti predicts ward-level flood risk from geospatial topology, rainfall time-series, and drainage data, giving civic authorities a window to act before the water arrives.

<br/>

### 🏥 MediNexus — Healthcare SaaS
![Stack](https://img.shields.io/badge/MERN-Full_Stack-20232A?style=flat-square&logo=react&logoColor=61DAFB)
![Stack](https://img.shields.io/badge/PostgreSQL-4169E1?style=flat-square&logo=postgresql&logoColor=white)

A hospital management system built to replace the paper-based chaos in mid-tier Indian hospitals. Patient records, appointments, billing, and cross-department workflows — all in one scalable platform.

<br/>

### ⚡ SunGrid Protocol — Decentralized Energy Trading
![Built in](https://img.shields.io/badge/Built_in-33_hours-FF6B35?style=flat-square)

Rooftop solar owners generate surplus energy with nowhere to sell it. SunGrid is a decentralized P2P renewable energy trading platform built in a single overnight hackathon sprint — concept to working demo in 33 hours.

<br/>

### 🤟 ISL Translator — Indian Sign Language AI
![Focus](https://img.shields.io/badge/Focus-Accessibility-2ECC71?style=flat-square)

~5 million deaf Indians can't fully use digital communication tools. ISL Translator uses real-time computer vision to recognize Indian Sign Language gestures and translate them to text. The people who need good technology most are the least served by it.
